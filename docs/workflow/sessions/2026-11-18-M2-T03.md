## Decision made before coding (developer, 2026-11-18)

The task text says `createGame` reveals the opening at `t = 0` and returns a game whose status is `'ready'`. Under the M1 rules any command that takes effect moves `'ready'` to `'playing'`, and an opening can clear the whole board. Option A1 was approved: `new Game(board)` is unchanged; `createGame` reveals the opening through an optional constructor parameter without events, log entry or status change; the status is `'ready'` until the first accepted command, and `'won'` at creation if the opening clears the board. Also approved: valid `t` means a non-negative safe integer, and `game.property.test.ts` generates non-decreasing `t`.
