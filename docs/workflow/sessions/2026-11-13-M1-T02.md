## Decisions and deviations

- Docs not changed: `DECISIONS.md` (no ADR, no open decision settled), `DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md` and `README.md` (nothing they say changed).
- ADRs: none. No open decision touched. No new canonical name besides `Board`; no new error class.
- Interpretation 1: the task says the constructor "dedupes" and that "duplicate mines [are] rejected". Resolved as: repeated indices collapse first, then the distinct count must equal `mineCount`. So `[5, 5]` for 2 mines is rejected, `[5, 5, 6]` for 2 mines is accepted.
- Interpretation 2 (follow-up from M1-T01): an out-of-range or non-integer mine index throws `RangeError`; a distinct-mine count that differs from `mineCount` throws `InvalidBoardConfigError` with a reason starting `mineCount`.
- Interpretation 3: every method validates its argument and throws `RangeError`; `indexOf` rejects `x >= width` instead of wrapping. `neighbors` returns a frozen, cached array in ascending order. The constructor stops reading once the list has more distinct mines than `mineCount`.
